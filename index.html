A few useful answers

Before you begin.

Still need a hand?

Can I start studying as soon as I sign up?

You can create your account right away. Access to assigned MCQs requires a complete profile and administrator approval. Your video courses appear according to your course enrollment.

How do I get access to a video course?

Ask your course admin about enrollment. If you have a course activation code, you can redeem it from the Video Courses area after signing in.

Is MedBank free?

The Android app is free to download. Course access and pricing are arranged separately with the course admin. Contact MedBank for the details that apply to your courses.

Can I use my account on my phone?

Yes. Use the website on your phone or download MedBank for Android. Sign in with the same account to continue your study progress. iOS and Huawei apps are coming soon.
