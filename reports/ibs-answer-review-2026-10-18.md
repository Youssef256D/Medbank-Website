## 3. Places where the book's key is wrong but MedBank is already right (no change needed)

For your information, in case the book is reprinted:

- **Pyloric sphincter is a physiological sphincter:** the book says True. It is an anatomical sphincter, so the answer is False.
- **Clear waste products (Lec 6 q20):** the book says Pulmonary artery. The answer is Renal vein.
- **Does not ossify:** the book says Epiphyseal plate. The answer is Gomphosis (the epiphyseal plate does ossify).
- **Usually ossifies with age:** the book says Secondary cartilaginous joint. The answer is Suture and primary cartilaginous.
- **Left lung is (3 lobes / shallow base / short / large):** the book says Large. The answer is Has a shallow concave base, since the right lung is the larger one.
- **The ejaculatory duct is formed by union of:** the book says Seminal vesicle and prostate. It is the vas deferens plus the seminal vesicle duct, so the answer is None.
- **Considered as a primary sex cell (Lec 7 q8):** the book says Secondary spermatocyte. The answer is Spermatogonium, which the book itself gives in Embryology Part 1.
- **The vestibule (Lec 7 q13):** the book says Gums and tongue. The answer is Gums and cheeks.
- **Which cavity replaces the blastocele:** the book says Amniotic. The answer is Primary yolk sac.
- **The part of chorion facing uterine cavity:** the book says Chorion frondosum. The answer is Chorion laeve.
- **Which part deepens inside the notochordal process:** the book says Primitive node. The answer is Primitive pit.
- **Blastocyst is formed of … blastomeres:** the book says 16, which is the morula. MedBank's 64 is right.
